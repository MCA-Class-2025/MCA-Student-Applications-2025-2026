# Leave and Attendance Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All full-time and part-time employees
**Department:** Human Resources

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the types of leave available to employees, the process for requesting leave, and the rules governing attendance at NimbusTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

## 2. Types of Leave

### 2.1 Casual Leave (CL)
Employees are entitled to **12 days** of casual leave per calendar year, credited at 1 day per month. Casual leave can be used for personal errands, family obligations, or short-notice absences. A maximum of 3 consecutive casual leave days may be taken at once without prior HR approval.

### 2.2 Sick Leave (SL)
Employees receive **10 days** of paid sick leave per year. For sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered practitioner must be submitted within 3 working days of returning to work.

### 2.3 Earned/Privilege Leave (EL)
Employees accrue **1.5 days** of earned leave per month (18 days annually), which can be accumulated up to a maximum of **45 days**. Earned leave must be applied for at least 5 working days in advance except in emergencies.

### 2.4 Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to **26 weeks** of paid maternity leave as per the Maternity Benefit Act, applicable for the first two children. For the third child onward, the entitlement is 12 weeks.

### 2.5 Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to **15 calendar days** of paid paternity leave, to be availed within 6 months of the child's birth.

### 2.6 Bereavement Leave
Employees may avail up to **5 days** of paid bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling).

### 2.7 Leave Without Pay (LWP)
If an employee exhausts all paid leave categories, they may request Leave Without Pay, subject to manager and HR approval. LWP beyond 30 days requires Director-level sign-off.

## 3. Leave Application Process

1. Employees must apply for leave through the HRMS portal at least 48 hours in advance for planned leave.
2. Emergency leave must be communicated to the reporting manager via email or phone before the start of the working day.
3. Leave requests are approved or rejected by the direct manager within 24 working hours.
4. Unapproved absences will be marked as "Leave Without Pay" and may result in disciplinary action if recurring.

## 4. Attendance Rules

- Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
- Employees are expected to log in and out via the biometric/HRMS attendance system daily.
- Three instances of unexplained late arrival (beyond 15 minutes) in a month will trigger an HR advisory note.
- Remote/hybrid employees must mark attendance through the online portal by 10:00 AM.

## 5. Public Holidays

The company observes **12 public holidays** per year as per the official holiday calendar published by HR every January. Employees required to work on a public holiday are entitled to compensatory leave.

## 6. Carry Forward and Encashment

- Casual Leave and Sick Leave **cannot** be carried forward to the next calendar year and lapse on December 31.
- Earned Leave can be carried forward up to the 45-day cap; leave encashment is permitted at the time of separation, calculated on the last drawn basic salary.

## 7. Contact

For queries regarding leave balances or policy clarifications, contact **hr-support@nimbustech.com** or raise a ticket on the HR helpdesk.
