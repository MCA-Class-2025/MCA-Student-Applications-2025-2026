RECOMMENDATION POLICY

Recommendations must follow SecureShare security rules.

Low Risk:
Allow the login and continue normal monitoring.

Medium Risk:
Allow the login, notify the administrator, recommend a password change, and monitor future login activity.

High Risk:
Immediately notify the administrator, investigate the user's recent activity, and review the account before allowing sensitive operations.

Blocked User:
Keep the account restricted until an administrator verifies the user's identity and security status.

All recommendations must be professional, concise, and consistent with SecureShare's Responsible AI policy.
