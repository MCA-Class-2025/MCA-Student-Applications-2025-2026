LOAN

A loan is money borrowed from a bank or financial institution that must be repaid over a specific period with interest.

Types of Loans:
1. Personal Loan
2. Home Loan
3. Education Loan
4. Vehicle Loan
5. Gold Loan
6. Business Loan

Important Terms:
• Principal – The original amount borrowed.
• Interest – Extra money paid to the lender.
• Tenure – Time allowed to repay the loan.
• EMI – Monthly payment made towards the loan.

Advantages:
• Helps meet financial needs.
• Enables purchase of expensive assets.
• Supports education and business growth.

Disadvantages:
• Interest increases total repayment.
• Late payments affect credit score.
• Defaulting on a loan may result in legal action.

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment)

EMI is the fixed amount a borrower pays every month to repay a loan.

An EMI includes:
• Principal Amount
• Interest Amount

Factors affecting EMI:
• Loan Amount
• Interest Rate
• Loan Tenure

A longer loan tenure reduces the monthly EMI but increases the total interest paid.

A shorter tenure increases EMI but reduces the total interest.

Paying EMI on time improves your credit score.

CREDIT SCORE

A credit score is a number that represents a person's creditworthiness.

In India, the credit score usually ranges from 300 to 900.

Credit Score Range:

750–900 : Excellent

650–749 : Good

550–649 : Average

Below 550 : Poor

Ways to Improve Credit Score:
• Pay EMIs on time.
• Pay credit card bills before the due date.
• Avoid taking multiple loans.
• Maintain a low credit utilization ratio.
• Do not default on loans.

Benefits of a Good Credit Score:
• Easier loan approval.
• Lower interest rates.
• Higher credit card limits.

FIXED DEPOSIT (FD)

A Fixed Deposit is an investment where money is deposited in a bank for a fixed period at a fixed interest rate.

Features:
• Guaranteed Returns
• Low Risk
• Fixed Interest Rate
• Suitable for Conservative Investors

Advantages:
• Safe investment.
• Predictable returns.
• Flexible tenure.
• Loan facility against FD.

Disadvantages:
• Premature withdrawal may attract penalty.
• Returns are lower than equity investments.

RECURRING DEPOSIT (RD)

A Recurring Deposit allows customers to deposit a fixed amount every month for a fixed period.

Benefits:
• Encourages regular savings.
• Fixed interest rate.
• Safe investment.
• Suitable for salaried employees and students.

Difference between FD and RD:

FD:
One-time investment.

RD:
Monthly investment.

INFLATION

Inflation is the increase in the prices of goods and services over time.

Effects of Inflation:
• Reduces purchasing power.
• Increases cost of living.
• Decreases the value of money.

Ways to Beat Inflation:
• Invest regularly.
• Increase income.
• Avoid unnecessary expenses.
• Maintain emergency savings.

TAX BASICS

Tax is a compulsory payment made by individuals and businesses to the government.

Types of Taxes:
• Income Tax
• Goods and Services Tax (GST)
• Property Tax

Why Taxes are Important:
• Development of roads.
• Education.
• Healthcare.
• Public services.
• National development.

Income Tax:
Income tax is charged on the income earned by an individual or business according to government rules.
