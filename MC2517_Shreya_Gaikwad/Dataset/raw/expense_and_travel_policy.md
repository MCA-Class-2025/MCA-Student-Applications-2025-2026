# Expense Reimbursement and Business Travel Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-POL-004
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees incurring business-related expenses
**Department:** Finance

## 1. Purpose

To establish clear guidelines for incurring, claiming, and approving business-related expenses and travel, ensuring responsible use of company funds.

## 2. General Expense Rules

- All expenses must be business-related, reasonable, and supported by original invoices or digital receipts.
- Expense claims must be submitted via the Finance Expense Portal within **15 days** of incurring the expense.
- Claims submitted after 60 days will not be processed without VP-level exception approval.
- Reimbursements are processed within 7 working days of manager approval, credited directly to the employee's registered bank account.

## 3. Travel Booking

- All domestic and international travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel partner (TravelDesk Inc.), not booked independently, except in documented emergencies.
- Flights: Economy class for domestic travel; Premium Economy for international flights exceeding 6 hours, for employees at Manager level and above.
- Advance booking is required at least 5 working days prior to travel to secure cost-effective fares, except for urgent client-driven travel.

## 4. Accommodation

- Hotel bookings should not exceed **INR 6,000/night** for metro cities and **INR 4,000/night** for non-metro cities, unless client site constraints dictate otherwise.
- Extended stays beyond 5 nights require pre-approval from the department head.

## 5. Daily Allowance (Per Diem)

| Travel Type | Domestic | International |
|---|---|---|
| Meals & Incidentals | INR 1,200/day | USD 40/day |
| Local Conveyance | INR 500/day | USD 15/day |

Per diem is paid regardless of actual spend, in lieu of itemized meal receipts, unless the employee opts for actual-expense reimbursement.

## 6. Local Conveyance (Non-Travel)

Employees using personal vehicles for official local travel (client visits, inter-office meetings) can claim mileage reimbursement at **INR 8/km**, capped at INR 2,000/month, with a logged trip sheet.

## 7. Client Entertainment

Client entertainment expenses (meals, hospitality) require pre-approval from the reporting manager for amounts exceeding **INR 3,000** per instance and must include the names of attendees and business purpose on the claim.

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are **not** reimbursable under any circumstance:
- Personal entertainment, spa services, mini-bar charges
- Traffic fines or penalties
- Alcohol (except pre-approved client entertainment)
- First-class flight upgrades
- Personal grooming or laundry beyond 5 consecutive travel days

## 9. Approval Hierarchy

- Expenses up to INR 10,000: Direct manager approval
- Expenses INR 10,001 – INR 50,000: Department head approval
- Expenses above INR 50,000: Finance Controller and Department Head approval

## 10. Corporate Credit Cards

Employees issued a corporate card must reconcile all transactions monthly through the Finance Portal. Misuse of the corporate card for personal expenses may result in card revocation and disciplinary action.
