## Is PCOD different from PCOS?
PCOD means polycystic ovarian disease. It is an older or alternative name often used for the condition called polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS); the names should not be presented as two reliably separate diagnoses or as a simple mild-versus-severe scale. If a clinician used PCOD in a report, ask what findings and diagnostic criteria they meant.

PCOS describes a pattern involving menstrual or ovulation changes, signs of higher androgen levels, and sometimes a polycystic ovarian appearance. Ovarian appearance alone does not establish the diagnosis, and some people diagnosed with PCOS do not have polycystic ovaries on ultrasound. A clinician considers symptoms and tests and excludes other possible causes.

In May 2026, the international guideline group adopted polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS) as the new name for the condition previously called PCOS. People and clinical documents may still use PCOS or PCOD, so it helps to ask which condition and criteria the clinician means.
