# IT Security and Acceptable Use Policy

**Document ID:** IT-POL-005
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors with system access
**Department:** Information Technology

## 1. Purpose

To protect NimbusTech's information assets, systems, and data from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss, and to define acceptable use of company IT resources.

## 2. Password and Authentication

- All company accounts must use passwords of at least **12 characters**, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all email, VPN, and cloud application access.
- Passwords must be changed every **90 days** and must not be reused across the last 5 passwords.
- Sharing of login credentials with any other person, including colleagues, is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Device Security

- All company-issued laptops must have disk encryption, endpoint antivirus, and the corporate mobile device management (MDM) agent installed and active at all times.
- Devices must be locked (screen-lock) whenever unattended, even for short periods.
- Loss or theft of a company device must be reported to the IT Helpdesk **within 2 hours** of discovery.

## 4. Data Classification and Handling

Data is classified into four levels:
- **Public** – No restriction on sharing.
- **Internal** – For employee use only; not for external sharing.
- **Confidential** – Restricted to specific teams; requires NDA for external sharing.
- **Restricted** – Highly sensitive (e.g., client PII, financial records, source code); access on a strict need-to-know basis, encrypted at rest and in transit.

## 5. Email and Phishing

- Employees must not click links or open attachments from unknown or suspicious senders.
- Suspected phishing emails should be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook/Gmail, which forwards the email to the Security Operations Center (SOC).
- The company conducts periodic simulated phishing tests; employees who repeatedly fail these tests are required to complete mandatory security training.

## 6. Software and Application Use

- Only software approved and installed via the IT self-service portal may be used on company devices.
- Installation of unauthorized or pirated software is strictly prohibited.
- Use of personal cloud storage (e.g., personal Google Drive, Dropbox) to store company data is not permitted.

## 7. Acceptable Use of Internet and Systems

- Company systems are provided primarily for business use. Reasonable personal use (e.g., checking personal email during breaks) is permitted provided it does not violate other policies or consume excessive bandwidth/storage.
- Accessing illegal, offensive, or non-business-appropriate content on company systems is strictly prohibited and subject to disciplinary action.

## 8. Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident (data breach, malware infection, unauthorized access) must be reported immediately to **security@nimbustech.com** or the 24x7 SOC hotline. Employees must not attempt to investigate or resolve security incidents independently.

## 9. Remote Access

Access to internal systems from outside the office network requires the company VPN client. Split-tunneling and use of unauthorized VPN/proxy tools is prohibited.

## 10. Consequences of Violation

Violations of this IT Security Policy may result in suspension of system access, disciplinary action, or termination, and in cases involving data breaches, potential legal action.
