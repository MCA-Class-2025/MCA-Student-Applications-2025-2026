# Code of Conduct Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-002
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and interns
**Department:** Human Resources & Compliance

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of professional behavior expected from every individual associated with NimbusTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. It is designed to foster a respectful, safe, and productive workplace.

## 2. Core Principles

### 2.1 Integrity and Honesty
Employees must conduct all business dealings with honesty and integrity. Falsification of company records, expense reports, timesheets, or performance data is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate termination.

### 2.2 Respect and Non-Discrimination
NimbusTech is committed to a workplace free of discrimination or harassment based on race, gender, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, marital status, or nationality. Any employee found violating this principle will be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

### 2.3 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or familial relationships that could create a real or perceived conflict of interest with their job duties, including relationships with vendors, clients, or competitors. Disclosures should be made to the HR/Compliance team via the annual conflict-of-interest declaration form.

### 2.4 Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential company information, client data, trade secrets, or proprietary technology to any unauthorized third party, both during and after employment. This obligation survives termination of employment for a period of 3 years.

### 2.5 Gifts and Entertainment
Employees may not accept gifts, hospitality, or favors from vendors or clients exceeding **INR 5,000** in value without prior disclosure to their manager and the Compliance team.

### 2.6 Use of Company Assets
Company equipment, software licenses, and IT resources are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work, violate other policies, or incur significant cost.

## 3. Anti-Harassment and POSH Compliance

NimbusTech maintains zero tolerance for sexual harassment in the workplace, in compliance with the POSH Act, 2013. An Internal Committee (IC) has been constituted to receive and investigate complaints confidentially. Complaints can be filed with the IC via **posh-committee@nimbustech.com**.

## 4. Social Media and Public Communication

Employees must not speak on behalf of the company in public forums, press, or social media without authorization from the Corporate Communications team. Personal social media use should not disclose confidential business information or bring the company's reputation into disrepute.

## 5. Substance-Free Workplace

Consumption or possession of alcohol or illegal drugs on company premises or during work hours is strictly prohibited, except at officially sanctioned company events where alcohol may be served responsibly.

## 6. Reporting Violations

Employees who witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct are encouraged to report it through:
- Direct manager or HR Business Partner
- The anonymous Ethics Hotline: 1800-XXX-XXXX
- Email: **ethics@nimbustech.com**

Retaliation against anyone who reports a violation in good faith is strictly prohibited and will itself be treated as a policy violation.

## 7. Disciplinary Action

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in a verbal warning, written warning, suspension, or termination of employment, depending on the severity and frequency of the violation, following due process managed by HR.
