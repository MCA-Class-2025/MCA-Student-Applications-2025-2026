# Remote Work and Hybrid Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-003
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2025
**Applies To:** Eligible full-time employees
**Department:** Human Resources

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the eligibility, expectations, and guidelines for employees working remotely or in a hybrid arrangement.

## 2. Eligibility

Remote/hybrid work is available to employees whose roles do not require constant physical presence (e.g., not applicable to facilities, security, or lab-based roles). Eligibility is determined by the reporting manager in consultation with HR, based on role requirements and performance history. New hires must complete **90 days** in an office-based setting before being considered for a remote arrangement, unless the offer explicitly states a remote role.

## 3. Work Arrangement Types

- **Fully Remote:** Employee works from a location outside the office at all times.
- **Hybrid:** Employee is required to work from office **3 days per week** (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday are designated anchor days for most teams).
- **Fully On-site:** Employee works from the office every working day.

## 4. Equipment and Reimbursement

- The company provides a laptop, headset, and necessary software licenses for remote work.
- Employees working remotely on a fully remote basis are eligible for a one-time home-office setup reimbursement of up to **INR 15,000**, and a monthly internet/utility allowance of **INR 1,500**.
- Reimbursement claims must be submitted with valid invoices via the Finance portal within 30 days of purchase.

## 5. Availability and Communication

- Remote employees must be available and responsive on company communication tools (Slack/Teams/Email) during core hours: **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM** local time.
- Cameras should be turned on for scheduled team meetings and client calls unless otherwise agreed.
- Employees must update their calendar status to reflect availability, breaks, or focus time.

## 6. Data Security for Remote Work

- Remote employees must use company-issued VPN when accessing internal systems.
- Use of personal devices for accessing company email or systems is prohibited unless enrolled in the company's Mobile Device Management (MDM) program.
- Sensitive documents must not be printed or stored on personal, unencrypted devices.

## 7. Performance Expectations

Remote work arrangements do not alter performance expectations, deliverables, or reporting timelines. Managers will conduct regular one-on-ones (at least bi-weekly) to ensure alignment and support.

## 8. Revocation

The company reserves the right to revoke remote work privileges if performance concerns arise, security guidelines are violated, or business needs change. Employees will be given a 2-week notice period wherever possible before transitioning back to full on-site work.

## 9. International Remote Work

Working remotely from a country other than the employee's registered work location is **not permitted** without prior approval from HR and Legal, due to tax, compliance, and data-residency implications. Requests for temporary international remote work (up to 30 days) can be submitted via the HR portal at least 3 weeks in advance.
